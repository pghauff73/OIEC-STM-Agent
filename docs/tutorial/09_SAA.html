SAA

Search qualified knowledge before inventing a replacement.

TestedDocumentation evidence
  • docs/tutorial/09_SAA.md
  • tests/test_docs_tutorials.py

What you will learn

You will distinguish retrieval of qualified known work from generation of a new candidate.

Everyday analogy

A technician searches a repair manual and failure log before inventing a procedure. A matching title is not enough; the operating context and qualification must also match.

New vocabulary

SAA means Searchable Algebra of Algorithms. Qualification states the evidence and boundary under which a record may be reused. Retrieval selects compatible records.

Diagram

The diagram compares the current context with algorithm, reasoning, and failure records before choosing reuse, adaptation, generation, or abstention.

Command or interaction

Use the retrieval sandbox to select a context and inspect why one known solution qualifies while another superficially similar result does not.

Expected output

The system returns a qualified algorithm, a qualified reasoning procedure, an adaptation candidate, a known failure, or an explicit gap.

What just happened?

Search happened before invention. Identity, semantics, evidence, boundaries, and failure history participated in the decision.

Try changing this

Change one context field and observe whether the record remains reusable, requires adaptation, or becomes ineligible.

Common mistake

Keyword similarity does not prove that two algorithms or failures are equivalent in the current context.
