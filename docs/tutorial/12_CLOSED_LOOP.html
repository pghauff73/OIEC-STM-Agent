Closed Loop

Promote and re-retrieve only evidence-qualified knowledge.

TestedDocumentation evidence
  • docs/tutorial/12_CLOSED_LOOP.md
  • tests/test_docs_tutorials.py

What you will learn

You will distinguish candidate qualification, governed promotion, canonical storage, and context-qualified re-retrieval.

Everyday analogy

A workshop updates its repair manual only after a procedure passes review. The update is useful only if future technicians can find the correct procedure for the same kind of machine.

New vocabulary

Promotion admits a qualified record under governance. Canonical knowledge has one content-addressed owner. Re-retrieval proves the stored improvement can be found again in context.

Diagram

The diagram follows candidate, evidence, promotion gate, canonical store, and context-qualified retrieval back into a later task.

Command or interaction

Advance the closed-loop fixture through qualification, promotion, and retrieval. Inspect the lineage and context check at every transition.

Expected output

The final state is CLOSED_LOOP_IMPROVEMENT_VERIFIED only when the promoted record is retrieved again under the intended qualification boundary.

What just happened?

The system proved more than local candidate success. It verified that governed knowledge storage and later reuse form a working loop.

Try changing this

Alter the retrieval context after promotion. Observe whether the record remains qualified, requires adaptation, or fails closed.

Common mistake

Writing a record to a store is not the same as proving that future retrieval will select it correctly.
