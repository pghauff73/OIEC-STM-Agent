# Business: Choosing Between Two Processes

## Problem

Two workflows promise faster delivery but use different quality measures and workloads.

## Governed analysis

OURD maps stakeholders, inputs, outputs, constraints, costs, and hidden dependencies. IURM freezes a representative workload and measures one declared change. IEPS checks quality, delay, rework, and limitations before a governed decision.

## Evidence status

**Theoretical teaching example.** It illustrates comparison structure and does not recommend a specific business process.

## Lesson

A process comparison is invalid when each candidate is measured on a different workload or when protected outcomes are omitted.
