# Cooking: Why Did the Bread Fail?

## Problem

A loaf is dense despite following a familiar recipe.

## Governed analysis

OURD maps ingredients, process, environment, equipment, timing, and measurement. IURM freezes the recipe and varies one justified dimension such as proofing time. The evidence gate prevents a broad claim when only one loaf and one condition were tested.

## Evidence status

**Theoretical teaching example.** The fixture illustrates controlled variation; it does not establish food-science results.

## Lesson

Changing many recipe variables at once can produce a better loaf without revealing why it improved.
