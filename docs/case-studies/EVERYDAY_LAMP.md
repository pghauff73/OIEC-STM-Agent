# Everyday: Why Will the Lamp Not Turn On?

## Problem

A kitchen lamp remains dark. Replacing the bulb once did not help.

## Governed analysis

OURD maps the bulb, fitting, switch, circuit, breaker, supply, and observation conditions. IURM chooses one controlled test. CFEL records that the bad-bulb hypothesis lost support under the observed configuration.

## Evidence status

**Theoretical teaching example.** The fixture is deterministic and does not diagnose real electrical systems. Real electrical work requires qualified human authority and safety procedures.

## Lesson

Problem mapping and failure memory prevent the same plausible but unsupported repair from being repeated indefinitely.
