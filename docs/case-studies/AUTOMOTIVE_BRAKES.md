# Automotive: Unexpected Brake Vibration

## Problem

A vehicle vibrates during braking, but only under some speeds and temperatures.

## Governed analysis

OURD maps wheels, rotors, pads, suspension, road, speed, temperature, load, and prior service. IURM identifies discriminating observations while safety boundaries exclude uncontrolled road experiments. Evidence gates prevent one inspection from becoming a universal diagnosis.

## Evidence status

**Theoretical teaching example.** It is not vehicle repair guidance. Inspection and testing require qualified technicians and safe facilities.

## Lesson

Scope, operating conditions, and authority matter as much as the suspected component.
