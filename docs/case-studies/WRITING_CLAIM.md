# Writing: Unsupported Claim in a Report

## Problem

A draft says a system is complete and safe because focused tests passed.

## Governed analysis

IEPS separates test evidence from broader certification. The evidence gate asks whether the test scope matches the claim scope. CFEL records contradictions such as missing full discovery, interrupted validation, or stale hashes.

## Evidence status

**Implemented governance pattern.** Repository plans and reports distinguish focused validation, full validation, human approval, and release status.

## Lesson

Writing quality cannot repair an evidence-scope mismatch. The claim must shrink or the evidence must expand.
