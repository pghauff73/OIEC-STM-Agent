Run an experiment

Hold a baseline, vary one dimension, and record observations.

ImplementedDocumentation evidence
  • docs/tasks/RUN_AN_EXPERIMENT.md

Goal

Use one controlled variation to reduce a named uncertainty.

Learning route

Complete T04 OURD, T05 IURM, and T11 A/B Evidence.

Experiment contract

Record the question, baseline, varied dimension, invariants, predicted outcomes, measurement method, source state, observations, and limitations before interpreting the result.

Misconception to avoid

An experiment that changes several coupled dimensions may produce a result but cannot attribute that result to one cause without additional structure.

Safe completion condition

The comparison is representative, source-bound, repeatable, and capable of falsifying at least one relevant hypothesis.
