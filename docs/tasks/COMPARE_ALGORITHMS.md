# Compare Algorithms

## Goal

Compare qualified algorithms or reasoning procedures under one declared context and evidence contract.

## Learning route

Complete T09 SAA, T10 Adaptation, and T11 A/B Evidence.

## Comparison contract

Hold model, input corpus, environment, budgets, measures, source state, and protected regressions constant unless a difference is the declared comparison dimension.

## Misconception to avoid

Popularity, name similarity, or one favorable metric does not establish equivalence or superiority.

## Safe completion condition

The verdict states qualification boundaries, improvement evidence, regressions, missing coverage, and whether reuse, adaptation, generation, or abstention is justified.
