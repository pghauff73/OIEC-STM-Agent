Understand a failure

Decode a refusal or failed expectation and identify the next safe step.

ImplementedDocumentation evidence
  • docs/tasks/UNDERSTAND_A_FAILURE.md

Goal

Decode a refusal, contradiction, regression, or failed expectation and determine the next safe action.

Learning route

Complete T03 Evidence and T08 CFEL.

Method

Use the Status Decoder for the exact uppercase status. Then inspect the trigger, source owner, violated invariant, evidence already present, evidence still required, and whether the context differs from earlier failures.

Misconception to avoid

Failure does not automatically mean “try again.” Repetition is justified only when relevant evidence, context, or the candidate has changed.

Safe completion condition

The next step is evidence-producing, context-changing, or explicitly abstaining rather than a disguised blind retry.
