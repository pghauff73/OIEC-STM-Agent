TUTORIALSFAILURE MUSEUM

SOURCE-LINKED · BUILT 2026-08-30

Each named system answers a recurring failure mode.

This is an architectural sequence, not a claim about historical invention dates. Every entry links the problem it addresses to current source owners.

01

Make intent inspectable

Problem: Broad human requests hide assumptions, scope, and ambiguity.

Architectural response: HRT records a reviewable interpretation before deeper modeling.

Sources: README.md, tools/docs_concept_catalog.py
02

Model the territory

Problem: A plausible first solution can hide missing objects and dependencies.

Architectural response: OURD decomposes the problem into orthogonal, uniquely identified components while preserving their relations, dependencies, boundaries, exclusions, and unknowns.

Sources: README.md, ourd/egcf/catalog.py
03

Learn through controlled variation

Problem: Changing several dimensions at once produces weak causal evidence.

Architectural response: IURM selects baselines, invariants, dimensions, and responses.

Sources: README.md, ourd_gui/views/iurm.py
04

Separate reasoning from mutation

Problem: A good idea is not yet an authorized, source-bound action.

Architectural response: EON binds exact targets, state, authority, evidence, tests, and rollback.

Sources: README.md, ourd/egcf/adapters/eon.py
05

Stop blind retries

Problem: A failed approach can recur with different wording but no new evidence.

Architectural response: CFEL records expectation, observation, collision, and next learning constraints.

Sources: README.md, ourd/cfel.py
06

Retrieve before reinventing

Problem: Qualified solutions and known failures are wasted when every task starts from generation.

Architectural response: SAA represents, qualifies, searches, adapts, and re-retrieves reusable structures.

Sources: docs/SAA_1_CANONICAL_IR.md, docs/SAA_11_3_11_4_SAA_12_CLOSED_IMPROVEMENT.md
